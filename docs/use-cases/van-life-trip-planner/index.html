Plan a van life road trip without losing track of who paid for gas

Short answer

Vacationist keeps a van road trip's shifting route on a shared calendar, splits fuel, campsites, and groceries the moment they happen, and keeps working with zero signal — syncing automatically once you're back in range.

The problem with planning a van life road trip

Road trip plans change daily — a stop gets skipped, a detour adds two nights, the route bends around weather. Whatever tool you're tracking the plan in needs to keep up without a full re-plan every time someone changes their mind. Then there's the money: fuel, campsite fees, groceries for the shared kitchen — small costs that pile up fast and are easy to lose track of when nobody wants to be "the one who brings up money" every single day. And the real problem underneath all of it: the places van life actually happens — backroads, mountain passes, remote campsites — are exactly where phone signal disappears.

How Vacationist handles it

Keep the route flexible with a calendar that updates as plans change

The shared calendar tracks each stop, so when the group decides to skip a town or add an extra night somewhere with good hiking, everyone sees the update instantly instead of hearing about it secondhand.

Split fuel, campsites, and groceries the moment they happen

Log each cost as it comes up — gas station, campsite fee, the grocery run for dinner — split equally or with custom amounts if not everyone's driving the same distance. Balances update live so nobody's running a mental tally three states in. See how expense splitting works.

A packing list that keeps the van organized for everyone

One shared, real-time list for gear, food, and supplies — so the van doesn't end up with three camp stoves and no bottle opener. Explore shared lists.

Works with zero signal, syncs when you're back in range

Votes, expenses, and list updates queue automatically when there's no connection — normal for backroads and remote campsites — and sync the moment a phone finds signal again. Nobody has to remember to "add it later."

What it costs

Free, with no ads. Voting, expense splitting, shared lists, and guest access for the whole crew cost nothing. A Pro tier exists for organizers running frequent or year-round trips, but it isn't required for a single road trip.

Frequently asked questions

Does it actually work without cell signal?

Yes. Vacationist is offline-first — votes, expenses, and list changes are saved locally and sync automatically once a device reconnects, which matters a lot on backroads and in national parks.

Can we track who's owed what over a multi-week trip?

Yes. Every cost is logged as it happens and balances update in real time, so you can settle individual amounts along the way or clear everything at once at the end of the trip.

Does it handle multiple currencies if the route crosses a border?

Vacationist supports basic multi-currency tracking, which covers most road trips. If you're converting frequently across several currencies, the handling is simpler than a dedicated finance app.

What if passengers change leg to leg?

Anyone can join the trip via the invite link at any point and start participating immediately — no need to re-invite the whole group when someone joins partway through.
