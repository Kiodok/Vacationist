Frequently asked questions

What is the best free alternative for splitting group trip costs?

It depends on whether you need only a ledger or the whole trip. For a pure ledger, several focused apps exist and are covered in the Splitwise alternatives list. For planning, voting and splitting in one place, Vacationist is free with no ads.

Do I need everyone in the group to install the same app?

Not with Vacationist — guests can join a trip through a single link and take part without creating an account, and there is also a web app. Requirements differ between apps, which is one of the rows compared on each list.

How are the alternatives chosen?

By what a group of friends or family actually needs on a trip: making decisions together, splitting costs fairly, keeping shared lists, and staying in sync. Each entry states its strengths and weaknesses rather than only its features.
