# Built for every kind of group trip

<p class="lede">Every group trip has the same three problems, whatever the occasion: deciding what to do, paying for it fairly, and getting everyone actually informed. Vacationist solves all three in one app — here's how it plays out for the trips people plan most.</p>

## Pick your trip

### [Bachelorette party planner →](/use-cases/bachelorette-party-planner/)

Vote on the itinerary, split the villa and the group dinners fairly, and get every bridesmaid in with one link — no app-store detour for the cousin who never signs up for anything.

### [Van life trip planner →](/use-cases/van-life-trip-planner/)

Keep the route flexible, split fuel and campsite costs the moment they happen, and stay organized offline — because signal on the road is never guaranteed.

### [Ski trip group budget →](/use-cases/ski-trip-group-budget/)

Split the chalet, lift passes, and gear rental fairly even when everyone's paying for different things, and vote on rest days versus resort days before the group chat turns into a debate.

### [Family reunion planner →](/use-cases/family-reunion-planner/)

Invite every generation with one link — nobody needs an app-store account, including grandma — and split venue and catering costs by household instead of an awkward even split.

### [Group camping trip →](/use-cases/group-camping-trip/)

Coordinate who's bringing the tent and the stove, split campsite fees and groceries as they happen, and keep planning offline-first when the campground has no signal.

### [Stag do planner →](/use-cases/stag-do-planner/)

Vote on activities so the best man isn't the tie-breaker for everything, split deposits and the group dinner fairly, and get every out-of-towner in with a single link.

## The pattern behind all of them

Whatever the trip, the same three tools do the work: [activity voting](/features/voting/) turns group indecision into an actual answer, [expense splitting](/features/expenses/) keeps money from becoming the awkward part, and guests can [join with a single link](/features/voting/) — no account, no app-store detour, no one left out of the plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Vacationist only for vacations?

No. It works for anything a group needs to coordinate and pay for together — bachelorette parties, ski weeks, family reunions, camping trips, road trips, and regular weekenders alike.

### Do all my guests need to create an account?

No — anyone can join a trip with a single invite link and fully participate (vote, add expenses, edit lists) without signing up for anything.

### Which use case is closest to mine if it's not listed here?

Most group trips are a mix of these patterns. Start with the one closest to your trip's shape — the same features (voting, splitting, lists, guest access) apply regardless of the occasion.
