# Vacationist vs. Troupe: which group trip app is right for you?

<p class="lede">Short answer: Troupe and Vacationist start from the same idea — a single app for a group to plan a trip, vote on plans, and split costs — so the feature lists look similar on paper. The real differences are in the details: how documents are secured, how easy it is for the least tech-savvy person in the group to join, and how the app behaves with no signal.</p>

## Where the apps genuinely overlap

If you've looked at both, you've probably noticed the pitch is similar: stop coordinating a trip across five apps, put voting, an itinerary, and expense splitting in one place. That overlap is real, and if a friend is already using one of the two for your trip, it's usually fine to go with what the group already has open.

## What makes Vacationist different

- **Encrypted travel documents, not just a planner.** Group trips mean sharing passport and ID details for bookings — usually via chat screenshot. Vacationist has a dedicated [AES-256 encrypted document vault](/features/travel-documents/) with biometric unlock and revocable, temporary organizer access. This isn't a common feature among group-trip apps.
- **Zero-friction guest joins.** Anyone can [join a trip via a single link](/features/voting/) and immediately vote, add expenses, and edit shared lists — no account, no app-store detour for the one friend who never gets around to signing up.
- **Offline-first by default.** Group trips happen on planes, ferries, and mountain roads with no signal. Vacationist queues votes, expenses, and list changes and syncs automatically on reconnect — for everyone, not as a paid add-on.
- **Genuinely free, no ads.** The core app — voting, [expense splitting](/features/expenses/), lists, chat — has no ads and no tracking. A Pro tier exists for larger or more frequent groups; it doesn't gate core coordination features.
- **Built in Switzerland with a privacy-first default.** For groups sharing financial and travel-document data, where and how the company handles privacy is part of the decision, not an afterthought.

## The realistic recommendation

If your group is already coordinating in Troupe and it's working, switching apps has its own cost — sticking with what everyone already opened is often the right call. If you're choosing fresh, the deciding factors are usually: do you need encrypted document storage for passports, does everyone in the group need to join without installing anything or creating an account, and do you want offline support without a paid tier. On all three, Vacationist has a clear, verifiable answer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Vacationist basically the same as Troupe?

The core pitch is similar — a group trip app with polls and expense splitting — but Vacationist differentiates on encrypted travel document storage, offline-first sync for every user, and no-account guest participation. Compare current feature lists directly, since both apps evolve.

### Can I switch a trip from Troupe to Vacationist partway through planning?

There's no direct import between the two. Starting a new Vacationist trip takes under a minute — create the trip, invite the group with one link, and rebuild the plan from there.

### Does Vacationist have polls like Troupe?

Yes — [activity voting](/features/voting/) works like a poll but with five response tiers (from "must do" to "group blocker") so the group's real preferences surface instead of a simple yes/no count.

### Which app is better for storing passport details for a group booking?

Vacationist — [encrypted travel documents](/features/travel-documents/) are AES-256 encrypted, unlocked with biometrics, and organizers get temporary, revocable access instead of a permanent chat screenshot.
